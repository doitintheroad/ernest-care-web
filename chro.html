THE ACCESS PARADOX

Why your leadership cohort ignores your standard benefit.

CHROs invest millions in wellness suites and digital mental health apps. Yet when senior leaders face high-stakes cognitive fatigue, unmanaged strain, or burnout, they bypass company offerings completely. Here is the structural breakdown:

01 · THE VISIBILITY FEAR

The Triage Stigma

Even with strict confidentiality clauses, executives believe internal benefits data can leak to peers or boards. If accessing care requires navigating HR-linked platforms, they choose silence.

02 · THE GENERALIST MISMATCH

Zero Executive Acumen

Standard networks match clients to junior therapists who tell high-performers to "turn off Slack at 5 PM." For an executive managing a turnaround or audit, this advice destroys clinical credibility.

03 · THE TOKEN LIMIT

The 3-Session Cliff

Standard benefits cut clients off after 3 or 5 sessions. For severe executive strain, terminating care mid-flight creates cynicism and forces leaders out-of-pocket, ruining utilization.
