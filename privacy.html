LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Ernest Care, we are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients' personal health information (PHI) in compliance with the Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) of Ontario, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and the strict regulatory guidelines established by the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO).

1. Collection of Information

We collect personal and health information necessary to provide safe and effective psychotherapy and corporate advisory services. This includes demographic data, clinical history, and billing information. All information is collected directly from you or your authorized representative.

2. Corporate Client Isolation (B2B)

For clients utilizing our Corporate Retainer or Executive services, please be explicitly aware: your employer has absolutely zero access to your clinical records, session notes, or diagnostic information. We provide fully anonymized, aggregate-only utilization data to corporate partners. Your individual participation and clinical data remain strictly confidential.

3. Storage and Security

All clinical records are maintained electronically on SOC-2 Type II compliant Practice Management Software hosted on secure Canadian servers. We do not store PHI locally on unsecured devices.

4. Limits of Confidentiality

Under CRPO and Ontario law, confidentiality is absolute, with the following mandatory exceptions: (a) Imminent risk of serious bodily harm to yourself or others, (b) Suspected abuse or neglect of a child under 16, (c) Suspected sexual abuse by a regulated health professional, or (d) A subpoena or court order.

5. Website Tracking

This website uses minimal, privacy-first analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. We do not use tracking pixels to correlate your identity with mental health inquiries.

Contact

For questions regarding this policy or to request access to your records, please contact the Health Information Custodian at daniel@ernest.care.
