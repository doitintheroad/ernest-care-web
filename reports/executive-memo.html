1. The Financial Mathematics of Executive Attrition

In high-stakes professional environments (law, private equity, and enterprise consulting), the loss of a senior equity partner is an operational crisis. Industry benchmarks (SHRM, NALP) consistently document that replacing an equity partner costs 200% to 400% of their base compensation, factoring in:

  • Direct Search & Lateral Recruitment Fees: $150,000 – $400,000+ per placement.
  • Client Relationship & Book-of-Business Attrition: Portable billings transferring to competitors ($1M – $5M+).
  • Institutional Knowledge & Associate Churn: Team disruption and lost productivity during vacancy.

A $26,400 annual retainer ($2,200/mo CAD) acts as an institutional loss-prevention policy. Retaining just one partner every 5 years yields an estimated 1,800%+ ROI on clinical investment.

3. Board Implementation & Invoicing Protocol

The retainer is structured for complete governance transparency and zero administrative friction:

  • Blind Invoicing: Invoices are delivered on standard Net-15 corporate terms stating "Executive Clinical Infrastructure Retainer". Zero Personal Health Information (PHI) or clinician notes appear.
  • Quarterly Utilization Telemetry: For cohorts where n ≥ 5, anonymized aggregate utilization data is provided to HR/Finance to verify program efficiency without compromising individual clinical confidentiality.
Regulatory & Statutory Governance: Practice regulated by the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO #17513). Compliant with Ontario Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA, 2004), Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA s. 32.0.1), and WSIB Bill 127 standards.
