Legal Counsel Summary & PHIPA Isolation

Ernest Care operates as a regulated clinical practice, not a software broker. Under Ontario's PHIPA, 2004, The designated Clinical Director serves as the sole Health Information Custodian (HIC). All clinical session notes, diagnoses, and attendance records are protected under statutory privilege. Zero Personal Health Information (PHI) is ever disclosed, billed, or shared with the client firm.

1. The Corporate Clinical Air-Gap Architecture

To eliminate privacy friction and shield the firm from employment liability, our retainer establishes a complete operational air-gap:

Node 01
Direct, Encrypted Partner Booking

Eligible leadership cohort members book sessions directly through a private, encrypted portal. No HR triage, approval tickets, or internal referral routing.

Node 02
Sole Health Information Custodian (HIC) Vault

All clinical records reside in an isolated, encrypted, PIPEDA/PHIPA-compliant health record repository under the statutory custody of the Health Information Custodian.

Node 03
Blind Net-15 Corporate Invoicing

Invoices are billed as a flat monthly retainer. The firm receives zero names, zero session counts per person, and zero clinical diagnoses.

2. Statutory Privilege & Subpoena Resistance

Unlike corporate executive coaches or unregulated performance consultants—who carry zero statutory privacy and whose notes are fully discoverable in litigation—Ernest Care clinicians are regulated health professionals under the Psychotherapy Act, 2007.

  • Discovery Insulation: Clinical therapy records are governed by common-law Wigmore privilege criteria and strict PHIPA confidentiality protections.
  • Mandatory Duty-to-Warn Boundaries: As with all regulated Ontario health professionals, statutory disclosure exceptions exist solely under immediate risk of severe bodily harm, child protection statutes, or court-ordered subpoena following formal judicial privilege review.

3. Anonymized Telemetry Standards

For client firms requiring board-level reporting, Ernest Care provides quarterly aggregate reports subject to strict threshold rules:

  • Minimum Cohort Threshold: Aggregate utilization data is only reported for cohorts with n ≥ 5 eligible participants to prevent re-identification.
  • Metric Scope: Telemetry is strictly limited to aggregate capacity utilization percentages and general workshop attendance numbers.
