THE EVIDENCE

Fit. Enough sessions.
The rest is usually packaging.

If you buy mental health benefits for leadership teams, vendor decks will sell modalities and apps. The outcome literature is plainer. Two factors keep showing up as reliable predictors of whether psychotherapy helps: whether the person and the clinician fit, and whether there are enough sessions to do real work.

01 · FIT

Fit is not an algorithm.

A real therapeutic alliance predicts outcomes more reliably than an app's brand. Matching engines optimize for roster coverage, not for whether a partner actually shows up.

02 · DEPTH

Session depth is not a token.

Sustained, high-pressure strain requires a clinical course of care. Token 3-session caps cut off treatment mid-flight, resulting in chronic burnout and zero trust.

03 · CAPACITY

Dedicated capacity. Real care.

The retainer is structured around named clinical availability and a shared monthly bank. Clinicians who understand high-stakes professional realities.

Program design informed by research on the working relationship (Flückiger et al., 2018) and on how many sessions people typically need (Howard et al., 1986, and subsequent routine-care reviews). Individual outcomes vary. This is not a guarantee of results. CRPO-regulated practice.

ONTARIO CONTEXT · NOT A CERTIFICATION

A documented access model. Not a legal defense.

Since 2018, Ontario workers may claim WSIB benefits for a diagnosed mental injury predominantly caused by a substantial work-related stressor (WSIA chronic mental stressBill 127 amended the Workplace Safety and Insurance Act so chronic mental stress can be compensable. Ordinary employer decisions (workload, hours, discipline, termination) are generally excluded. A clinical retainer is not WSIB coverage and is not a defense.). Separately, OHSA s. 32.0.1 requires a workplace harassment policy. A generic EAP that senior people will not use is not a finished support model. This carve-out is documented access. It is not WSIB compliance, not a substitute for counsel, and not a promised clinical result.

QUESTIONS FROM HR AND PEOPLE OPS

Straight answers.

Does this replace our existing EAP?
It can, or it can sit alongside one as a carve-out for leadership and senior professionals who are not using the standard benefit. Most organizations start as a complement, then reassess once utilization data shows the gap it is closing.
How is pricing structured?
Corporate retainers start at $2,200/month CAD for Core (10 sessions/month, $220 overage) and $4,000/month for Enterprise (20 sessions, $200 overage). There is no per-session claims process. Bespoke work is quoted. The $2,500 workshop is credited in full toward a retainer signed within 14 days.
How is confidentiality protected from HR?
Sessions are never disclosed to HR or leadership. What comes back is aggregate, de-identified utilization data only, and only when the cohort is large enough (n≥5) that a person cannot be read out of the number.
How fast can someone access care?
48 business hours from first contact to intake consultation for anyone on a Core or Enterprise retainer. If that window is missed, the program receives a service credit. No matching algorithm, no queue.
What if someone is in crisis?
This is not a crisis service. Anyone in immediate crisis should call or text 988 or go to the nearest emergency room. Ernest Care is built for sustained, high-functioning strain, not acute emergencies.
Is this only for executives?
The primary design is for leadership and senior professionals in high-responsibility, client-facing, or high-stakes roles, the group standard EAPs tend to reach least effectively. Organizations define the eligible cohort themselves.
Why are there no client logos on this site?
We do not publish our client roster. When an organization retains us for executive clinical support, their participation is protected with the exact same legal rigor as our clinical privacy. Our corporate relationships are completely off the record.
